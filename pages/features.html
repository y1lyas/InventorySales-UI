Business Overview

A simple inventory and sales business flow with categories, product stock control, query support, and basic sale creation.

Category Management

Organize Products by Category

Create and manage product categories to keep inventory organized and easy to search.

  • Product grouping: Categories help classify items clearly.
  • Query support: Search products by category or related filters.
  • Business clarity: Easier stock review and product lookup for business users.
Product Stock

Add and Adjust Inventory

Create products, increase stock, and decrease inventory as items are sold or returned.

  • Stock control: Add quantity when new inventory arrives.
  • Stock reduction: Decrease quantity when products are sold.
  • Product queries: Check stock levels and product details anytime.
Sales Workflow

Create Basic Sales

Record sales in the system and automatically reduce inventory for each sale.

  • Simple sale flow: Create a sale record without complex payment details.
  • Inventory impact: Sale creation lowers stock levels immediately.
  • No payment processing: Business focus remains on stock and sales records.
